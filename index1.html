Head teachers message

It is with great joy and pride that I welcome you to the 2025 academic year at Gombe Secondary School! Whether you are joining us for the first time or returning to continue your journey, I am thrilled to embark on this year of growth, discovery, and achievement together. At Gombe, we are steadfast in our commitment to nurturing academic excellence, integrity, and leadership in every student. Our school’s rich history and tradition of excellence are built on a foundation of holistic education—where classroom learning, extracurricular pursuits, and character development go hand in hand. This year, we celebrate the remarkable success of our 2024 candidates , who achieved a 98% pass rate in national examinations, a testament to the dedication of our students and staff
